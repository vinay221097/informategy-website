// How it works

A 2-minute session in 6 simple steps

// One assessment

One assessment. Five core skills.

Whatever the subject or topic, every session is measured consistently across the same five communication skills.

Pronunciation

Speak clearly and with correct sounds.

Fluency

Speak smoothly with a natural flow.

Grammar

Use correct grammar and sentence structure.

Vocabulary

Use the right words in the right context.

Confidence

Speak with clarity, confidence and expression.
