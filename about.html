About Informategy

Who We Are

We Build AI That Actually Runs Businesses

Informategy exists to close the gap between AI potential and operational reality. We design and deploy AI systems that replace manual work, execute workflows, and scale operations — reliably.

Our Journey

Built From Real Problems. Refined in Production.

Informategy was not created as a trend-driven AI studio or a consulting experiment. It was built from direct exposure to operational friction — teams scaling faster than their systems could support.

Across Europe and India, we worked with founders and operators facing the same issue: manual processes compounding as businesses grow. AI existed, but execution was missing.

Our response was simple: build AI systems that don’t assist work — they complete it.

Our Philosophy

AI Is Not a Feature. It’s an Operator.

Execution Over Intelligence

A smart model that doesn’t execute workflows is useless. Our AI agents trigger actions, update systems, and close loops end-to-end.

Production First

Everything we build is designed for real usage — with monitoring, failure handling, and human overrides from day one.

ROI or Nothing

If an AI system doesn’t reduce cost, save time, or unlock scale — it doesn’t ship.

Founder’s Note
