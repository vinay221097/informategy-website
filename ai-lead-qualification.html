Sales Automation

AI Lead Qualification & Sales Automation Platform

An end-to-end AI system that qualified inbound leads across chat, email, and CRM — routing only high-intent prospects to human sales teams.

Client Context

The client was a B2B services company generating a high volume of inbound leads from website chat, contact forms, email campaigns, and ads. While lead volume was strong, the sales team was overwhelmed by low-intent inquiries, repetitive qualification calls, and inconsistent CRM data.

The result: slow response times, missed opportunities, and sales teams spending more time filtering leads than closing deals.

Key Challenges
  • High inbound lead volume with low qualification accuracy
  • Manual lead screening consuming sales bandwidth
  • Inconsistent data across chat, email, and CRM
  • Delayed follow-ups reducing conversion rates

The AI Solution

AI Lead Intelligence

An LLM-powered agent analyzed inbound conversations and form data to understand intent, urgency, budget signals, and use-case relevance.

Automated Qualification

Leads were automatically scored, categorized, and enriched with context before being routed — eliminating manual screening.

CRM & Sales Routing

High-intent leads were pushed into the CRM with structured notes, while low-intent inquiries were handled autonomously.

Measured Impact

The AI system replaced the entire manual lead qualification layer, allowing sales teams to focus exclusively on high-value conversations.

  • 60% reduction in manual lead handling effort
  • Faster first response times across all channels
  • Higher sales team productivity per rep
  • Improved CRM data quality and consistency
Why It Worked

The system was designed to execute decisions, not just assist. By embedding AI directly into sales workflows and enforcing structured outputs, the platform delivered predictable, measurable business outcomes.
