Healthcare & Wellness

Nutrition & Patient Data Intelligence

A healthcare and nutrition provider was losing continuity of care to fragmented data — patient history, nutrition plans, lab reports and follow-ups all lived in disconnected tools. Informategy built an AI-driven data-intelligence system that unifies everything into a single operational workflow, generating personalized insights automatically and scaling data-backed patient engagement without adding headcount.

Business context

Nutrition and wellness providers managed patient history, reports and ongoing engagement across multiple disconnected tools and formats. Every practitioner stitched the picture together by hand, which limited continuity of care, slowed personalization, and made consistent follow-up nearly impossible at scale.

Core challenges

Manual analysis of every record, fragmented data with no single source of truth, inconsistent and easily-missed follow-ups, and a hard scaling ceiling tied directly to how many practitioners were on staff.

Solution deployed

Informategy deployed a production-grade AI system that consolidated patient data, generated insights automatically and triggered follow-ups — all inside the tools the team already used, with no disruptive migration.

Unified data layer

Patient records, plans and reports consolidated into a single, structured intelligence layer every workflow can draw from.

Automated insights

AI-generated summaries, progress reports and contextual recommendations produced the moment new data arrives.

Follow-up automation

Intelligent reminders and engagement workflows triggered automatically, so no patient falls through the cracks.

Operations

Operational Workflow Automation

High-volume internal workflows were being throttled by manual handoffs, inconsistent routing and delayed follow-ups. Informategy deployed AI execution agents that orchestrate these workflows end to end — triggering actions, updating systems, escalating only true exceptions and closing loops automatically across teams — replacing operational drag with reliable, scalable execution.

Business context

Operations teams ran high-volume, repetitive workflows across support, staffing and internal service functions. Execution depended heavily on human coordination — people moving work between systems, chasing updates and manually closing loops.

Core challenges

Manual handoffs slowed everything down, drove up cost per task, and produced inconsistent outcomes that only worsened as volume scaled.

Solution deployed

Informategy deployed an AI-driven workflow-execution system that orchestrates tasks end to end — routing work, updating systems of record, and surfacing only genuine exceptions to a human.

Automated routing
